| Internal account | Initial login     | Initial password | Default role       | After profile setup    |

| user1            | user1.hostelx.in  | user1            | **Admin – Kartik** | kartik.hostelx.in      |
| user2            | user2.hostelx.in  | user2            | User               | e.g. prince.hostelx.in |
| user3            | user3.hostelx.in  | user3            | User               | e.g. ankit.hostelx.in  |
| user4            | user4.hostelx.in  | user4            | User               | User's chosen name     |
| ...              | ...               | ...              | ...                | ...                    |
| user50           | user50.hostelx.in | user50           | User               | User's chosen name     |

**One important improvement:** don't actually change `user2.hostelx.in` into `prince.hostelx.in` in the database. Keep `user2` as the permanent internal account and use `prince.hostelx.in` as the user's **display identity**. That makes your Admin system much safer and prevents account conflicts.
